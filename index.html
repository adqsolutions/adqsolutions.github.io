All Training Modules

  • Internal Auditor Course on ISO 9001
    Learn principles and practices of auditing Quality Management Systems against ISO 9001 requirements.
  • Internal Auditor Course on IATF16949
    Master automotive QMS auditing techniques aligned with IATF 16949 standards and customer-specific requirements.
  • Internal Auditor Course on ISO 14001
    Develop specialized skills to evaluate and audit Environmental Management Systems for sustainable compliance.
  • Internal Auditor Course on ISO45001
    Understand how to effectively audit Occupational Health and Safety management systems.
  • Internal Auditor Course on ISO50001
    Learn frameworks for auditing Energy Management Systems to drive process and energy efficiency.
  • Advanced Product Quality Planning (APQP)
    A structured method for defining and establishing the necessary steps to ensure a product satisfies the customer.
  • Production Part Approval Process (PPAP)
    Standardized risk-mitigation process to ensure suppliers understand all customer engineering design records and specifications.
  • Failure Modes and Effects Analysis (FMEA)
    A systematic, proactive method for evaluating a process to identify where and how it might fail.
  • Statistical Process Control (SPC)
    Use statistical techniques to control a process or production method, reducing variation and increasing efficiency.
  • Measurement System Analysis (MSA)
    Mathematical method of determining the amount of variation that exists within a specific measurement process.
  • Manufacturing Process Audit
    A deep dive into evaluating shop-floor manufacturing processes for efficiency, compliance, and defect prevention.
  • Problem Solving Techniques
    Master essential analytical frameworks like 5-Why and Fishbone to identify root causes and implement lasting solutions.
  • Global 8 Disciplines (G8D)
    A comprehensive, structured problem-solving methodology used heavily by engineers to isolate and permanently correct recurring defects.
  • 7 QC Tools – Basic/ Old
    Fundamental graphical techniques (e.g., Pareto, Histograms, Check Sheets) critical for troubleshooting quality-related issues.
  • 7 QC Tools – New
    Advanced management and planning tools used to organize subjective data, structure conceptual ideas, and solve complex problems.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers about our QA consulting, training, and digital QMS services.

What quality assurance consulting services does ADQ Solutions offer?

We provide QMS implementation and auditing (ISO 9001 / IATF 16949), vendor and supplier development, root cause problem solving (8D, 5-Why, Fishbone, CAPA), workforce training, and custom digital QA dashboards and complaint management portals for manufacturing enterprises.

Which industries and locations does ADQ Solutions serve?

We're based in Nagpur, Maharashtra, and work with automotive and industrial manufacturing companies across MIDC industrial hubs and pan-India, specializing in powertrain products and automotive components.

Can ADQ Solutions help with IATF 16949 and ISO 9001 certification readiness?

Yes. We conduct internal audits, gap assessments, SOP standardization and internal auditor training to prepare manufacturing plants for IATF 16949 and ISO 9001 certification and surveillance audits.

Does ADQ Solutions provide digital tools, not just consulting?

Yes. Alongside on-ground consulting, we build lightweight digital QMS dashboards and customer complaint management portals that track rejection rates, inspection trends, non-conformances and CAPA timelines in real time.
